Overview

PromptCraft teaches prompt engineering — the craft of writing effective instructions for AI like Claude, GPT, and Gemini — as an interactive course rather than a wall of articles. Twelve lessons go from fundamentals to advanced patterns, each paired with quizzes and good-vs-bad prompt examples, and a playground lets learners practise straight away with ready-made templates.

Progress is the hook: XP for every completed activity, a daily streak counter, and twelve achievements to unlock. All progress persists locally — no accounts needed.
